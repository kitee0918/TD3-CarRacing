# Source and contribution notice

This repository builds on [Yanran Zhang's TD3-CarRacing project](https://github.com/Zhangyr2022/TD3-CarRacing), whose 2023 source is released under the MIT license retained in [LICENSE](LICENSE).

The `legacy/nov_2025/` scripts and the November 2025 commit history come from the course project formerly hosted at `https://gitee.com/MHLee/td3_car.git`. The historical commits have multiple authors; their author and commit dates are preserved. The `legacy/oct_2025/` scripts are a selected snapshot of local work dated October 29 through November 2, 2025. Those files had no corresponding Git commits in the local copy and were imported in the current publication commit, without backdating.

The new `src/td3_carracing/` package, evaluation protocol, and documentation are a later publication cleanup. Historical scores are identified separately from results produced by this package. Contributions by an individual course member should be described using the original commit authorship and project records rather than assigning the entire history to one person.
